JINEN Inc. Tokyo

JINEN brings Japanese families into global transactions, and global capital and companies into Japan.

We work through a network of family offices and investors in Japan and overseas, alongside licensed professionals in law, tax, real estate and securities, so that families and companies on both sides can transact with confidence.

Business

Real estate

Off-market commercial buildings, hotels, data centre sites and former factory land in Greater Tokyo, and residences, townhouses and ryokan in Tokyo, Kyoto, Niseko and Karuizawa, from a single asset to a portfolio. Many of these assets are offered privately by Japanese families planning for inheritance and succession. The family of our chairman, Arata Nakatsuji, has held and managed real estate for generations, and JINEN works through those relationships. Viewings through to closing are handled by licensed real estate firms.

Secondaries and pre-IPO

Secondary and pre-IPO shares in leading U.S. private companies, for families in Japan and Asia. Arata co-invests his own capital in the same transactions. JINEN introduces families to licensed firms and does not take orders or execute trades.

Japan tours / Events & parties

Each year Arata takes a few guests to places in Japan closed to the public and introduces them to the people behind them, from an Urasenke tea master to a Noh actor of the Kongō school. He also hosts JINEN-AN, an invitation-only circle of about 30 members. We arrange private dinners, receptions and parties for visiting families, investors and companies. Travel is booked through licensed travel agencies.

The Unseen Japan

Japan market entry

We bring overseas companies into Japan and support their first year: partners, first customers, hiring and licensing. Brand and creative work is delivered with Ultra Super New, one of Japan’s leading creative agencies, based in Tokyo.

Partner Ultra Super New

Japanese art

Works from Edo-period masters to modern nihonga, introduced to buyers privately or through the auction houses. Arata is the tenth generation of the house of Maruyama Ōkyo, the eighteenth-century Kyoto painter regarded as the father of modern Japanese painting.

People

Chairman

Arata Nakatsuji

Arata is a member of the founding family of OMRON, headquartered in Kyoto, and the tenth generation of the house of Maruyama Ōkyo. He spent five years in Silicon Valley, where he founded AI ventures in music and healthcare in San Francisco and led a multinational team of 40. He is co-founder and CEO of SAKRA.

Founding Partner

Hidehisa Morita

Hide is a third-generation member of the family of Sony co-founder Akio Morita, and the eighteenth generation of the Morita sake-brewing family, whose brewery was founded in 1665. Between the ages of 17 and 21 he worked under Yasushi Akimoto as a producer and manager for AKB48 and Nogizaka46. In 2022 he founded Queri Inc., which connects creators and fans around the world, and he serves as its CEO. At JINEN he works with overseas families and investors.
